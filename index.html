About the app

ELLA Travel is a travel comparison app for Arabic-speaking travelers in Iraq and the Middle East. It searches flights, hotels and car rentals, shows prices, durations, stops and baggage information, and sends you to trusted booking websites and airlines to complete your booking.

FlightsCompare by price, duration and stops, with baggage details.
HotelsCompare hotel prices across major booking sites.
Car rentalCompare rental companies at your destination.
Trip infoWeather, currency and country information.

عن التطبيق

ELLA Travel تطبيق أندرويد لمقارنة أسعار تذاكر الطيران والفنادق وتأجير السيارات من مواقع حجز متعددة، للمسافرين في العراق والشرق الأوسط. يعرض الأسعار والمدة والتوقفات ومعلومات الحقائب، ثم ينقلك إلى موقع الحجز أو شركة الطيران التي تختارها لإكمال الحجز.

Privacy policy

  • ELLA Travel does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as names, emails or payment details.
  • Your searches, saved flights and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Search requests are sent to travel data providers to get prices, weather and destination information.
  • When you open a booking site, that site's own privacy policy applies. Some links are affiliate links, which may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • You can delete all app data at any time from the app settings.
